# Channel audit checklist

Work through each section. Mark every line as **observed** (you can see the
evidence) or **assumed** (inference or user-reported). Never present assumed
numbers as observed metrics.

## 1. Positioning
- One-sentence description of the channel's niche and audience.
- Primary value proposition: what does a viewer get from subscribing?
- Tone and persona (educational, entertainment, hybrid).

## 2. Cadence and catalog
- Upload frequency over the last 10–20 videos (regular vs. sporadic).
- Long-form vs. Shorts mix.
- Series or playlists that recur, and how they are structured.

## 3. Packaging (titles + thumbnails)
- Recurring title patterns (numbered lists, questions, curiosity gaps, keywords).
- Thumbnail conventions: faces, text overlays, color palette, consistency.
- Title/thumbnail alignment with the actual content (over-promising?).

## 4. Hook and retention (only what you can observe)
- First 5–15 seconds: how is attention earned?
- Structure: cold open, chapters, pacing, on-screen text.
- Calls to action and end screens.

## 5. Production quality
- Video resolution and aspect ratio norms.
- Audio clarity, music use, captions/subtitles.
- Motion, editing style, and brand consistency across videos.

## 6. Gaps and opportunities
- Underserved topics adjacent to the channel's strengths.
- Formats the channel has not tried that fit its audience.
- Quick wins vs. larger production bets.

## 7. Recommendation
- Top 3–5 prioritized actions.
- For each: expected effort, why it fits, and a rough production route
  (Explore → Choose → Control → Ship → Repair → Finish → QA).
